-----------
Pallet Town
-----------

- Begin the adventure in Pallet Town and head to Professor Oak’s Lab.
- Choose your starter Pokémon: Bulbasaur (Grass), Charmander (Fire), or Squirtle
(Water).
- Your rival Blue automatically chooses the starter with type advantage.
- The first rival battle begins immediately after selecting your Pokémon.
- Recommended setup: open the Options menu and set Text Speed to Fast.
- Battle Style can remain on Shift for convenience or be switched to Set for
challenge play.
- Trainers:

  * RIVAL BLUE
    - Pokémon: Starter Pokémon (Lv 5)
    - Tips: Use your attack move repeatedly. Growl can reduce incoming damage.

-------
Route 1
-------

- Route 1 connects Pallet Town and Viridian City.
- Wild encounters include Pidgey and Rattata.
- A man near the start of the route gives you a free Potion.
- Ledges along the route allow quick return south toward Pallet Town.
- Lowering a Pokémon’s HP increases capture success when using Poké Balls.
- Available Pokemon

  * Pidgey

  * Rattata

----------------
Viridian City
----------------

- Heal your team at the Pokémon Center. Healing is free.
- The PC system stores Pokémon beyond your six-member party.
- Visit the Poké Mart to collect Oak’s Parcel for Professor Oak.
- Return the parcel to Professor Oak in Pallet Town.
- Oak rewards you with the Pokédex and five Poké Balls.
- Daisy gives you the Town Map afterward.
- After the Pokédex is obtained, the Viridian City old man demonstrates how to
catch Pokémon.
- Viridian Gym is locked and cannot be entered yet.
- Items

  * Oak’s Parcel

  * Pokédex

  * Poké Balls (x5)

  * Town Map

--------
Route 22
--------

- Route 22 lies west of Viridian City.
- This area introduces the first optional rival battle.
- Battling wild Pokémon here can provide useful early experience.

- Available Pokemon

  * Rattata

- Trainers:

  * RIVAL BLUE
    - Pokémon: Pidgey (Lv 9), Starter Pokémon
    - Tips: Sand Attack may lower accuracy. Heal if necessary during the battle.

-------
Route 2
-------

- Route 2 connects Viridian City with Pewter City.
- The eastern side path is blocked by a tree that requires Cut.
- The northern entrance leads directly into Viridian Forest.
- NPCs warn that Viridian Forest functions as a maze.
- Available Pokemon

  * Rattata

---------------
Viridian Forest
---------------

- Viridian Forest is an early maze filled with Bug-type trainers.
- Charmander performs very well here because Ember is super effective against
Bug-types.
- Weedle may inflict poison with Poison Sting, so carrying Antidotes is helpful.
- Trainer battles trigger if you enter a trainer’s line of sight.
- Pikachu can be captured here early in the game.
- Available Pokemon

  * Caterpie

  * Weedle

  * Pikachu

- Items

  * Potion

  * Antidote

- Trainers:

  * BUG CATCHER
    - Pokémon: Weedle (Lv 6), Caterpie (Lv 6)
    - Tips: Fire-type moves defeat Bug Pokémon quickly.

* BUG CATCHER

    - Pokémon: Weedle (Lv 7), Weedle (Lv 7), Kakuna (Lv 7)
    - Tips: Focus on leveling your starter early rather than spreading EXP
widely.

* BUG CATCHER

    - Pokémon: Caterpie (Lv 7), Caterpie (Lv 8)
    - Tips: Fire-type moves remain the most efficient strategy.

* BUG CATCHER

    - Pokémon: Metapod (Lv 7), Caterpie (Lv 7), Metapod (Lv 7)
    - Tips: Metapod has high defense but weak offense.

* BUG CATCHER

    - Pokémon: Weedle (Lv 9)
    - Tips: Continue using Fire attacks to end battles quickly.

-----------
Pewter City
-----------

- Pewter City hosts the first Gym challenge.
- Status effects such as Sleep or Paralysis make Pokémon easier to capture.
- The Pewter Museum of Science is optional and contains fossil displays.
- Entry costs 50G for a child ticket.
- Traded Pokémon gain experience faster but may disobey inexperienced trainers.
- Pewter Mart sells useful items for upcoming dungeon exploration.
- Items (Mart)

  * Awakening — 250G

  * Burn Heal — 250G

  * Escape Rope — 550G

  * Repel

----------
Pewter Gym
----------

- The Gym guide recommends adjusting party order before battles.
- Charmander faces difficulty against Rock-types.
- Bulbasaur and Squirtle have an easier matchup thanks to Grass and Water moves.
- Healing at the Pokémon Center before challenging Brock is recommended.
- Trainers:

  * JR. TRAINER♂
    - Pokémon: Geodude (Lv 10), Sandshrew (Lv 11)
    - Tips: Grass and Water attacks defeat both Pokémon quickly.

----------------
Gym Leader Brock
----------------

- Brock specializes in Rock-type Pokémon.
- Charmander users may need extra levels or support Pokémon to win.
- Losing the battle only costs a small amount of money, so retrying is safe.
- Trainers:

  * GYM LEADER BROCK
    - Pokémon: Geodude (Lv 12), Onix (Lv 14)
    - Tips: Grass and Water attacks defeat Brock easily. Charmander may require
extra levels or support Pokémon.
After the Battle

- Defeating Brock grants the Boulder Badge.
- The Boulder Badge allows the HM move Flash to be used outside battle.
- Brock awards TM34 (Bide).
- TMs in Red and Blue are single-use.
- Charmander evolves into Charmeleon at Lv 16.
- Heal your team before continuing the journey toward Mt. Moon.

--------
Mt. Moon
--------

- Mt. Moon lies east of the Route 3 Pokémon Center.
- It is a good place to save before entering.
- Geodude, Zubat, and Paras are common encounters here.
- Mankey is a useful weakening option when trying to catch Geodude.
- Zubat is a decent backup party member at this stage, especially around Lv 10.
- Paras is useful because Stun Spore can help with catches and future support
roles.
- Repels can be useful if repeated encounters become annoying.
- Clearing optional trainers in Mt. Moon often gives enough EXP to avoid
separate grinding later.
- After clearing optional paths, you can safely return to the Pokémon Center
outside the western entrance before taking the required route through the cave.
- Available Pokemon

  * Geodude

  * Zubat

  * Paras

  * Clefairy

- Items

  * Paralyze Heal

  * Potion

  * Rare Candy

  * Escape Rope

  * Moon Stone

  * Antidote

Navigation / Route Notes

- Upon entering Mt. Moon from the west side, go up and then left early to begin
optional exploration.
- If you continue left from the early section, you can fight an optional Bug
Catcher.
- After that, going up and to the left leads to a Paralyze Heal.
- You can leave Mt. Moon entirely after early catches and optional exploration
to heal at the Pokémon Center and update your team.
- Re-entering, the actual forward route begins by going right from the entrance
instead of left.
- Continue north and then up-right.
- In the large room with a visible ladder, the ladder itself is not the main
route yet if you want to clear optional content first.
- From that large room:

  * Going down-left leads to an optional Super Nerd.

  * Potion is next to him.

  * Going back down-right leads to a Rare Candy.

  * Going up leads to an optional Bug Catcher.

  * Continuing up-right gives an Escape Rope.

  * Going farther up-left leads to an optional Lass.

  * Continuing left through the loop area leads to a ladder where Paras can be
found.

- If you take that ladder down, you reach an optional lower area:

  * Paras can be encountered there.

  * Going left/up through that area leads to an optional Team Rocket Grunt.

  * Continuing right from that grunt leads to a Moon Stone.

- That whole lower Rocket section is optional.
- After finishing the optional western-side content, it is efficient to
backtrack completely to the Pokémon Center outside Mt. Moon, heal, save, and
then re-enter to take the required path cleanly.
- On the second pass through Mt. Moon:

  * Enter from the west side again.

  * Go up.

  * Pass the trainer area to the right.

  * Continue up.

  * Go left.

  * Go down to reach an optional Youngster.

  * Continue upward from there to an optional Hiker.

  * Going up-left past him leads to a Moon Stone.

- Back near the large room, the second ladder down leads to another optional
Team Rocket Grunt.
- After clearing those optional sections, take the path past the Hiker and
continue toward the northwest side.
- Go down the next ladder.
- In the new area:

  * Going up leads to a Revive.

  * Going right is the required route.

- Take the required route to the right, then go down the stairs to the Rocket
section.
- After the first required Team Rocket Grunt:

  * Go up the stairs.

  * Go right.

  * Go down.

  * Follow the path downward, then left as it loops around.

  * Continue up to the second required Team Rocket Grunt.

- After the second required Team Rocket Grunt, continue upward to the Super Nerd
and the fossil room.
- After choosing a fossil, go up and left past him.
- Continue along the path to pick up an Antidote.
- Then go up the ladder, and up another ladder, to exit Mt. Moon onto Route 4.
- Trainers:

  * BUG CATCHER
    - Pokémon: Caterpie (Lv 10), Weedle (Lv 10)
    - Tips: Fire-type moves are effective.

* SUPER NERD

    - Pokémon: Magnemite (Lv 11), Voltorb (Lv 11)
    - Tips: Ground-type moves are very effective.

* BUG CATCHER

    - Pokémon: Weedle (Lv 10), Kakuna (Lv 10)
    - Tips: Fire-type moves remain effective.

* LASS

    - Pokémon: Clefairy (Lv 14)
    - Tips: Clefairy has good bulk for this stage.

* TEAM ROCKET GRUNT

    - Pokémon: Rattata (Lv 13), Zubat (Lv 13)
    - Tips: Straightforward battle.

* YOUNGSTER

    - Pokémon: Rattata (Lv 13), Rattata (Lv 13)
    - Tips: Fast but fragile opponents.

* HIKER

    - Pokémon: Geodude (Lv 11), Geodude (Lv 11), Onix (Lv 11)
    - Tips: Water and Grass moves are very effective.

* TEAM ROCKET GRUNT

    - Pokémon: Rattata (Lv 14), Ekans (Lv 14)
    - Tips: Straightforward battle.

* TEAM ROCKET GRUNT

    - Pokémon: Sandshrew (Lv 15), Zubat (Lv 15)

* SUPER NERD

--------------
Route 4 (East)
--------------

- After leaving Mt. Moon on the eastern side, you re-emerge on Route 4.
- There is a small grass patch here with catches similar to the western side.
- TM05 Roar can be picked up here.
- Mega Punch and Mega Kick move tutors are also nearby.
- Spearow remains one of the useful catches here and can later help with Fly
utility.
- An NPC reminds you that Brock is respected, not just feared.
- Another NPC mentions tripping over a Geodude.
- The nearby Pokémon Center is a good place to recover from Mt. Moon and
reorganize the team.
- Available Pokemon

  * Spearow

  * Nidoran♂

- Items

  * TM05 Roar

-------------
Cerulean City
-------------

- Cerulean City is the next major stop after Mt. Moon.
- Heal at the Pokémon Center and reorganize your team after the cave.
- The Bike Shop is located on the southwest side of town.
- The Bicycle costs 1,000,000G and cannot be bought yet.
- A trade house west of the Pokémon Center offers a trade later.
- Several NPCs discuss Bill and his reputation as a Pokémaniac.
- Traded Pokémon grow faster, though their obedience can depend on badges.
- The northeast route is blocked by an officer due to a robbery, with implied
Team Rocket involvement.
- An NPC explains that Repel works better when your strongest Pokémon is first
in the party.
- Rare Candy is explained as an item that raises a Pokémon by one level
instantly.
- Suggested restock before continuing:

  * at least 10 Poké Balls

  * Potions

  * Repels
- Blue appears immediately north of Cerulean City before Nugget Bridge.
- After the rival battle, Blue mentions Bill.
- Trainers:

  * RIVAL BLUE
    - Pokémon: Pidgeotto (Lv 17), Starter Pokémon (Lv 18), Abra (Lv 15), Rattata
(Lv 15)
    - Tips: Pidgeotto falls quickly to strong attacks. The starter is usually
the toughest matchup. Abra is fragile. Winning here also points you toward Bill.

------------------
Sea Cottage (Bill)
------------------

- Sea Cottage is the house at the end of Route 25.
- NPCs nearby describe the cape as a famous date spot.
- Inside, Bill has accidentally fused himself with a Pokémon.
- Use the PC to activate the Cell Separation System and restore him.
- Bill rewards you with the SS Ticket.
- The SS Ticket is needed later to board the S.S. Anne in Vermilion City.
- Bill also mentions that the S.S. Anne has many trainers on board.
- After meeting Bill, use the shortcut route back toward Cerulean City.
- Items

  * SS Ticket

------------
Cerulean Gym
------------

- Misty’s Gym is in Cerulean City.
- The Gym guide recommends Grass-types such as Ivysaur or Paras, and
Electric-types such as Pikachu, against Misty.
- If you are using Charmeleon, you can still win by overleveling, using support
Pokémon, or brute-forcing with repeated attempts.
- Saving before and after Gym Leader battles is strongly recommended.
- Trainers:

  * SWIMMER
    - Pokémon: Horsea (Lv 16), Shellder (Lv 16)
    - Tips: Shellder is Water-type. Charmeleon can still win here through level
advantage.

* JR. TRAINER♀

    - Pokémon: Goldeen (Lv 19)
    - Tips: Optional extra EXP before Misty.

----------------
Gym Leader Misty
----------------

- Misty specializes in Water-type Pokémon.
- Staryu and Starmie are her main threats.
- Starmie is the real challenge because it hits hard and can outspeed many
Pokémon at this stage.
- Paras can support with Stun Spore.
- Revive and switch tactics are valid if you want to brute-force the battle.
- Losses only cost a small amount of money, so repeated attempts are acceptable.
- Trainers:

  * GYM LEADER MISTY
    - Pokémon: Staryu (Lv 18), Starmie (Lv 21)
    - Tips: Grass and Electric are ideal. Charmeleon can still win through level
advantage and persistence.
After the Battle

- Defeating Misty grants the Cascade Badge.
- The Cascade Badge allows higher-level Pokémon to obey you.
- This also applies to traded Pokémon up to that level.
- Cut can now be used outside of battle.
- Misty awards TM11 (BubbleBeam).
- Heal and save after the battle before continuing north.

Vermilion City
--------------

- Vermilion City is your next major stop after Route 6.
- Heal at the Pokémon Center and save periodically.
- The Fishing Guru in the northwest U-shaped house gives you the Old Rod.
- The Old Rod mainly catches Magikarp here.
- Magikarp evolves into Gyarados at Lv. 20 and can become a useful Water/Flying
addition later.
- The Pokémon Fan Club chairman gives you the Bike Voucher after listening to
him talk about Rapidash.

- In this run, that trade is completed and the player receives Farfetch’d named
CH’DING.
- Lt. Surge’s Gym is still blocked by a small tree until Cut is obtained.
- NPCs mention mail exchange, Pidgey delivering letters, and Route 12 being
blocked by a sleeping Snorlax.
- Route 12’s current detour eventually leads toward Rock Tunnel and Lavender
Town.
- An Awakening can be picked up on the eastern side approach near Route 12.
- The Bike Shop sells a Bicycle for 1,000,000G, which you cannot afford
normally.
- NPCs also mention pollution, Grimer, Machop flattening land for construction,
and Bill’s reputation.

- Poliwhirl → Jynx trade is mentioned later elsewhere, not completed here
