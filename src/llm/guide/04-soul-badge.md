# 4 badges: win the Soul Badge

Goal: beat Koga in `FuchsiaGym`. First finish the Pokémon Tower, which is what gets you
the Poké Flute, and the Poké Flute is what unblocks the road south.

Route: `LavenderTown` > `PokemonTower1F` up to `PokemonTower7F` > `LavenderTown` > `Route12` >
`Route13` > `Route14` > `Route15` > `FuchsiaCity` > `FuchsiaGym`.

1. `PokemonTower1F` is the cave-looking entrance in the north-east of `LavenderTown`.
   Bring the Silph Scope. The rival fights you on 2F, and floors 3F to 6F are Channelers
   whose Gastly and Haunter are Ghost/Poison. On 5F there are healing tiles in the
   middle of the floor, which you can stand on as often as you like.
2. On 6F a level 30 Marowak attacks as you approach the stairs. **You cannot fight it
   without the Silph Scope**; without one the game will not let you past at all.
3. Beat the three Rockets on 7F and talk to Mr. Fuji, who takes you home and gives you
   the **Poké Flute**.
4. Go south from `LavenderTown` on to `Route12`. **A sleeping Snorlax blocks the path.**
   Stand next to it and use the Poké Flute from the bag to wake it into a battle at
   level 30; it is worth catching, and there are only two in the game.
5. Keep south and west through `Route13`, `Route14` and `Route15` into `FuchsiaCity`.
   These are long routes with a lot of Trainers on them.
6. The second Snorlax is on `Route16`, west of `CeladonCity`, and blocks that road the
   same way. `Route16` into `Route17` also needs the Bicycle: the gatehouse guard turns
   you back without it, and `Route17` is a long one-way cycle south to `Route18` and
   back round to `FuchsiaCity`.
7. `FuchsiaGym` has invisible walls; the way through is up the east side of the room.
   Koga has Koffing 37, Muk 39, Koffing 37 and Weezing 43, all pure Poison, so Psychic
   and Ground moves hit hard. Most of the gym's other Trainers are Psychic types, which
   only Bug beats, so a strong neutral physical attack is usually the better answer.
   Weezing knows Selfdestruct and Toxic.

The Soul Badge raises Speed by 12.5% and is what allows Surf to be used outside battle.
HM03 (Surf) itself is in the Safari Zone, which is the next chapter, so take a Pokémon
that can learn Surf with you.
