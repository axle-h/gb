# 6 badges: win the Volcano Badge

Goal: beat Blaine in `CinnabarGym`. The gym is locked, and the key is at the bottom of
the Pokémon Mansion across the island.

Route: `FuchsiaCity` > `Route19` > `Route20` > `CinnabarIsland` > `PokemonMansion1F`
down to `PokemonMansionB1F` > `CinnabarGym`. The short way there is to Fly to `PalletTown` and surf
south down `Route21` instead.

1. Surf south out of `FuchsiaCity`. `Route19` and `Route20` are open water with a long
   line of Swimmers on them, and they lead west to `CinnabarIsland`.
2. Optional, and the only chance at it: `SeafoamIslands1F`, halfway along `Route20`.
   Articuno is on B4F. The current on B3F sweeps you along and cannot be surfed
   against; **the way to stop it is Strength**, pushing four boulders down the holes
   above it. Without Strength there is no point going in. ⚠️ **Strength is armed once per
   map and switched off again by every map change**, so use the field move afresh on each
   floor before pushing anything; a push before that moves nothing and reports nothing.
3. On `CinnabarIsland`, `CinnabarGym` is locked from the outside. `CinnabarLab`, the
   building on the east side, will turn a fossil into a Pokémon, so bring the Dome or
   Helix Fossil you chose in Mt. Moon.
4. The Pokémon Mansion is the burnt-out building in the north-west. It is four floors of
   barricades opened by **secret switches hidden in statues and furniture**: hit a
   switch and a wall somewhere else on the floor moves. The floors are also connected
   by holes you fall through. TM14 (Blizzard) and TM22 (SolarBeam) are both in here.
5. The **Secret Key** is in a room on B1F, and it is the only thing you actually need.
   Once you have it, Dig or an Escape Rope gets you out without walking back.
6. `CinnabarGym` is a series of doors that each ask a yes or no question about the
   game. Answer correctly and the door opens; answer wrong and you fight the Trainer
   behind it, which is only a delay.
7. Blaine has Growlithe 42, Ponyta 40, Rapidash 42 and Arcanine 47, all pure Fire.
   Water, Rock and Ground all hit for double, and you should have Surf, Rock Slide and
   Earthquake by now. Arcanine's Fire Blast will take out anything that is not resistant.

The Volcano Badge raises Special by 12.5%.
