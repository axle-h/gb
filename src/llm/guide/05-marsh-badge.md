# 5 badges: win the Marsh Badge

Goal: beat Sabrina in `SaffronGym`. On the way you pick up the last two HMs and clear
Team Rocket out of Silph Co.

Route: `SafariZoneGate` > the Safari Zone > `FuchsiaCity` > `WardensHouse` >
`SaffronCity` > `SilphCo1F` up to `SilphCo11F` > `SaffronGym`.

1. The Safari Zone is north of `FuchsiaCity`. Entry costs ₽500 and buys 30 Safari
   Balls. **You get a step limit, not a time limit**, and running out puts you back at
   the gate, so go for what matters first. Normal Poké Balls do not work in here.
2. Both things you need are in `SafariZoneWest`: the **Gold Teeth**, and the man in
   `SafariZoneSecretHouse` who gives you **HM03 (Surf)**. ⚠️ You cannot go straight
   there. The Centre's western exit is across water you cannot yet surf, so the only
   land route is the long way round: `SafariZoneCenter` > `SafariZoneEast` >
   `SafariZoneNorth` > `SafariZoneWest`.
3. Back in `FuchsiaCity`, give the Gold Teeth to the Warden in `WardensHouse`, east of
   the Pokémon Center, and he gives you **HM04 (Strength)**. You now have every HM.
   Teach Surf to something: from here on, water is a road.
4. Optional but worth it: `PowerPlant` is reachable only by surfing north from
   `Route10`, and Zapdos is sitting at the far end of it. There is exactly one. Several
   of the item balls in there are Voltorb and Electrode that attack when touched.
   `FightingDojo`, beside `SaffronGym`, gives you a choice of Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan.
5. Silph Co. is the tall building in the middle of `SaffronCity`, eleven floors of
   Rockets. The **Card Key** opens every locked barrier in there, and without it most of
   the building is shut. ⚠️ It is on 5F, but you cannot simply walk to it: it sits in a
   pocket walled off from the rest of the floor, and the only way in is to *arrive* on
   the teleport pad beside it. Take the lift to 5F, step on that pad to land on
   `SilphCo9F`, then step straight back on to the pad you arrived on; that puts you down
   on 5F inside the pocket.
6. The floors are also linked by teleport pads that do not go where you would guess.
   The pad in the middle of 3F lands you on 7F, where the rival fights you, and a man
   there gives you a level 15 Lapras. From 7F another pad reaches 11F, where Giovanni
   is: Nidorino 37, Kangaskhan 35, Rhyhorn 37, Nidoqueen 41. Afterwards talk to the
   president in the room to the north for the **Master Ball**, the only one in the game.
   There is a nurse on 9F who heals you for free.
7. `SaffronGym` is a grid of rooms joined by teleport pads. Sabrina is in the middle:
   Kadabra 38, Mr. Mime 37, Venomoth 38, Alakazam 43. In this generation Psychic is
   beaten only by Bug, and Bug moves are weak, so the practical answer is a fast, hard
   physical attacker rather than a type advantage.

The Marsh Badge makes traded Pokémon obey up to level 70.
