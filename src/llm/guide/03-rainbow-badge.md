# 3 badges: win the Rainbow Badge

Goal: beat Erika in `CeladonGym`. Getting there means crossing Rock Tunnel and then
clearing Team Rocket out of the Game Corner.

Route: `CeruleanCity` > `Route9` > `Route10` > `RockTunnel1F` > `RockTunnelB1F` >
`Route10` > `LavenderTown` > `Route8` > `Route7` > `CeladonCity` > `GameCorner` >
`RocketHideoutB1F` down to `RocketHideoutB4F` > `CeladonGym`.

1. Leave `CeruleanCity` east through `CeruleanTrashedHouse`. A tree blocks `Route9` and
   you need Cut to get past it.
2. `RockTunnel1F` is pitch dark. Flash makes it readable, and Flash is HM05, given by
   Oak's aide in the `Route2` gatehouse once you have ten species in the Pokédex. It is
   two floors and fifteen Trainers, coming out further down `Route10`. Heal at
   `RockTunnelPokecenter` first.
3. Carry on south to `LavenderTown`. `PokemonTower1F` is here but you cannot finish it
   yet: a ghost blocks the way and you need the Silph Scope, which steps 6 to 8 are
   about. Come back after this chapter.
4. Head west along `Route8`, through the Underground Path, along `Route7` and into
   `CeladonCity`.
5. In `CeladonCity`, two errands: buy a drink from the `CeladonMartRoof` vending
   machines, because the guard in the `Route7` gatehouse will not let you into
   `SaffronCity` until you hand him one. Then `Route16`, west of the city: Cut the tree,
   and the woman in the house past the gatehouse gives you **HM02 (Fly)**.
6. `GameCorner`, south-west of the Pokémon Center. Beat the Rocket standing in front of
   the poster at the back of the room, then examine the poster: it opens a hidden
   staircase into `RocketHideoutB1F`.
7. The hideout is four floors. `RocketHideoutB2F` and `RocketHideoutB3F` are arrow
   tiles that slide you until something stops you, and **you do not have to work them
   out**: the action menu's routes already know where every slide ends, and a slide is
   your walk being carried out, not a failure. Take the stairs down to
   `RocketHideoutB4F`, beat the Rocket there, and then **talk to him a second
   time**: his after-battle line is what drops the **Lift Key**, and until you have
   heard it there is nothing on the floor to pick up.
8. Now go back up to `RocketHideoutB2F` and board `RocketHideoutElevator` from there,
   not from `RocketHideoutB1F`, whose lift door stays shut until the Rocket guarding it
   is beaten. Ride to `RocketHideoutB4F`. That lands you in a room
   walled off from Giovanni; beat both Rockets in it and the wall comes down. Giovanni
   has Onix 25, Rhyhorn 24 and Kangaskhan 29, and leaves the **Silph Scope** on the
   floor when he goes.
9. To get out, ride the lift to `RocketHideoutB2F` and take the stairs up from there.
   `RocketHideoutB1F` is two halves split by a wall, only the northern one has the
   `GameCorner` staircase, and the lift's stop there is in the southern.
10. `CeladonGym` needs Cut twice over: trees seal the gym entrance from the city, and
    the inside is a garden whose paths are blocked by more of them. Both grow back when
    the map reloads, so beating Erika walls you into the garden and you cut your way out
    again. Erika has Victreebel 29, Tangela 24 and Vileplume 29: all Grass, two of them
    also Poison. Fire, Flying, Ice and Psychic all hit hard. Two of the three can sleep
    or poison you, so carry cures.

The Rainbow Badge makes traded Pokémon obey up to level 50 and is what allows Strength
to be used outside battle, for pushing boulders, once you find HM04.
