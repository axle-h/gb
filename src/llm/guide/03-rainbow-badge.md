# 3 badges: win the Rainbow Badge

Goal: beat Erika in `CeladonGym`. Getting there means crossing Rock Tunnel and then
clearing Team Rocket out of the Game Corner.

Route: `CeruleanCity` > `Route9` > `Route10` > `RockTunnel1F` > `RockTunnelB1F` >
`Route10` > `LavenderTown` > `Route8` > `Route7` > `CeladonCity` > `GameCorner` >
`RocketHideoutB1F` down to `RocketHideoutB4F` > `CeladonGym`.

1. Leave `CeruleanCity` east through `CeruleanTrashedHouse`. A tree blocks `Route9` and
   you need Cut to get past it. The route itself is nine Trainers.
2. `RockTunnel1F` is pitch dark. Flash makes it readable, and Flash is HM05, given by
   Oak's aide in the `Route2` gatehouse once you have ten species in the Pokédex. The
   tunnel is two floors, fifteen Trainers and no items, and comes out further down
   `Route10`. Heal at `RockTunnelPokecenter` before going in.
3. Carry on south to `LavenderTown`. `PokemonTower1F` is here but you cannot finish it
   yet: a ghost blocks the way and you need the Silph Scope, which is at the end of
   step 6. Come back after this chapter.
4. Head west along `Route8`, through the Underground Path, along `Route7` and into
   `CeladonCity`.
5. In `CeladonCity`, in this order:
   - `CeladonDiner`: the man in the top-left corner gives you the **Coin Case**.
   - `CeladonMansion1F`: upstairs, an Eevee sits in a Poké Ball on a desk upstairs.
   - `CeladonMart3F`: TM18 from the counter. A Poké Doll on `CeladonMart4F`. On
     `CeladonMartRoof` buy drinks from the vending machines. The girl there trades a
     Fresh Water, a Soda Pop and a Lemonade for TM13 (Ice Beam), TM48 (Rock Slide) and
     TM49 (Tri Attack). **Buy one spare drink**: the guard in the `Route7` gatehouse
     will not let you into `SaffronCity` until you give him one.
   - `Route16`, west of the city: Cut the tree, and the woman in the house past the
     gatehouse gives you **HM02 (Fly)**.
6. `GameCorner`, south-west of the Pokémon Center. A Rocket stands in front of the
   poster at the back of the room; beat him and he leaves. Then examine the poster to
   open a hidden staircase into `RocketHideoutB1F`.
7. The hideout is four floors of spin tiles, which slide you until you hit a stop tile.
   A Rocket on B4F drops the **Lift Key**, which is what makes the lift work. Behind
   the gate on B4F is Giovanni: Onix 25, Rhyhorn 24, Kangaskhan 29. Beating him gets
   you the **Silph Scope**.
8. `CeladonGym` needs Cut twice over: trees seal the gym entrance from the city, and
   the inside is a garden whose paths are blocked by more of them. Both grow back when
   the map reloads, so beating Erika walls you into the garden and you cut your way out
   again. Erika has Victreebel 29, Tangela
   24 and Vileplume 29: all Grass, two of them also Poison. Fire, Flying, Ice and
   Psychic all hit hard. Two of the three can put you to sleep or poison you, so carry
   cures.

The Rainbow Badge makes traded Pokémon obey up to level 50 and is what allows Strength
to be used outside battle, for pushing boulders, once you find HM04.
