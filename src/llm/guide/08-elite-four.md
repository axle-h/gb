# 8 badges: beat the Elite Four and win the game

Goal: cross Victory Road and beat the five Trainers at `IndigoPlateau`. Winning the
last one ends the game.

Route: `ViridianCity` > `Route22` > `Route23` > `VictoryRoad1F` > `VictoryRoad2F` > `VictoryRoad3F` >
`IndigoPlateauLobby` > the five rooms.

1. The rival fights you in the grass on `Route22` on the way through.
2. `Route23` is a long climb north past a row of checkpoints, each of which reads your
   badges and refuses you without the right one. You have all eight, so all of them
   let you by. Part of the route is water, so you need Surf to get up it.
3. Victory Road is three floors of boulder puzzles. Each floor has a switch in the
   ground held down by a boulder: **use Strength, push a boulder on to the switch, and
   a barrier somewhere else on that floor opens**. Nothing here works without Strength.
   ⚠️ **Strength is armed once per map and cleared by every map change**, so use the
   field move again on each floor before pushing; a push before that does nothing at all.
   Moltres is on 2F if you want it.
4. `IndigoPlateauLobby` has a Pokémon Center, a mart and a PC. **Heal, buy and save
   here**, because going down the stairs starts five battles in a row with no chance to
   shop and no Pokémon Center between them. Losing sends you back to the lobby and you
   start the five again from the beginning.
5. Lorelei: Dewgong 54, Cloyster 53, Slowbro 54, Jynx 56, Lapras 56. Water and Ice.
   Electric and Grass hit most of them for double; Fighting handles the Ice.
6. Bruno: Onix 53, Hitmonchan 55, Hitmonlee 55, Onix 56, Machamp 58. Fighting and Rock.
   Psychic beats the Fighting types outright; Water and Grass beat the two Onix.
7. Agatha: Gengar 56, Golbat 56, Haunter 55, Arbok 58, Gengar 60. Ghost and Poison,
   and they lean on Hypnosis, Confuse Ray and Toxic rather than damage. Psychic hits
   all five for double. Ground beats everything except Golbat, which is Flying.
8. Lance: Gyarados 58, Dragonair 56, Dragonair 56, Aerodactyl 60, Dragonite 62. Ice is
   the answer to every one of them, and Electric handles Gyarados and Aerodactyl. Four
   of the five know Hyper Beam.
9. The Champion is the rival: Pidgeot 61, Alakazam 59, Rhydon 61, then two of
   Arcanine, Exeggutor and Gyarados at 61 and 63, and his starter fully evolved at 65.
   **Which two of those three he has depends on the starter you took** in Oak's lab. It
   is the hardest fight in the game and there is no healing before it, so go in with
   full HP and full PP.

Beating him ends the game: the Hall of Fame records your team and the credits roll.
