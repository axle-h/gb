# 1 badge: win the Cascade Badge

Goal: beat Misty in `CeruleanGym`.

Route: `PewterCity` > `Route3` > `Route4` > `MtMoon1F` > `MtMoonB1F` > `MtMoonB2F` >
`Route4` > `CeruleanCity` > `Route24` > `Route25` > `BillsHouse` > `CeruleanCity` >
`CeruleanGym`.

1. `Route3`, east out of `PewterCity`, is a line of eight Trainers and nothing else.
   Fight them; the high teens is about right for this gym.
2. `MtMoonPokecenter` is at the north end of `Route4`. Heal, then take the cave beside
   it.
3. Mt. Moon is three floors of ladders and Team Rocket. Two things there matter: a Moon
   Stone in the north-west of 1F, and the Super Nerd at the far end of B2F, who lets
   you take the Dome Fossil or the Helix Fossil. You get one and never both. Watch for
   the Rocket with a level 16 Raticate: Hyper Fang hits hard.
4. Leave east onto the far half of `Route4` and drop south over the ledge into
   `CeruleanCity`. **That ledge is one way and there is no way back to Mt. Moon for a
   long time**, so finish anything there first.
5. The rival fights you in `CeruleanCity`: Pidgeotto 18, Abra 15, Rattata 15, and his
   starter at 17.
6. Go north over the bridge on `Route24`, through five Trainers and a Rocket, then east
   along `Route25` to `BillsHouse`. Talk to Bill, use his PC, talk to him again: he
   gives you the S.S. Ticket, which is the only way aboard the ship at Vermilion later.
7. `CeruleanTrashedHouse` has a hole knocked in its back wall. Go in the front and out
   the back into the yard behind it: a Rocket is standing there, and beating him gives
   you TM28 (Dig), 100 power and an escape from any cave. That yard is also the only
   way east out of the city, so you will come back through this house later.
8. `CeruleanGym`: a Swimmer, a Jr. Trainer, then Misty with Staryu 18 and Starmie 21,
   both Water. Grass is the answer, because it resists her attacks and hits for double.
   Electric hits as hard but takes full damage back.

The Cascade Badge makes traded Pokémon obey up to level 30, and is what allows Cut to
be used outside battle once you have the HM for it.
