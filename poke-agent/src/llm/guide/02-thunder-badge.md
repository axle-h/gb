# 2 badges: win the Thunder Badge

Goal: beat Lt. Surge in `VermilionGym`. First get aboard the S.S. Anne, because the
ship is where HM01 (Cut) comes from and Cut gates most of what follows.

Route: `CeruleanCity` > `Route5` > `UndergroundPathRoute5` > `Route6` >
`VermilionCity` > `VermilionDock` > the S.S. Anne > `VermilionGym` > back to
`CeruleanCity`.

1. `Route5` south of `CeruleanCity` leads into the Underground Path, which comes out on
   `Route6`. `Route6` is a line of six Trainers and nothing else.
2. In `VermilionCity`, two people are worth finding. The Fishing Guru in the house by
   the Pokémon Center gives you the Old Rod if you tell him you like fishing. The
   Chairman at the back of `PokemonFanClub` gives you a Bike Voucher for sitting
   through his story.
3. Show the S.S. Ticket at `VermilionDock` and board the ship. Work through the cabins
   for the Trainers, then fight the rival on 2F: Pidgeotto 19, Raticate 16, Kadabra 18
   and his starter at 20. The Captain is on 3F and gives you **HM01 (Cut)**.
4. **Stepping off the ship after taking Cut sends it away for good**, so do everything
   on board first. There is nowhere to heal on the ship.
5. Teach Cut to something you do not mind carrying it forever; an HM move can never be
   unlearned. Cut is a weak 50-power move, so a spare Pokémon is the usual choice.
6. **A cuttable tree seals the way to `VermilionGym`**, which is the real reason Cut
   comes before this badge. ⚠️ **A cut tree grows back whenever the map reloads**, and
   beating Surge reloads it — so stepping out of the gym leaves you shut inside the same
   little enclosure with the tree back. Cut it again. That is true of every cut tree in
   the game, not just this one.
7. Inside, the door to Surge is behind a trash-can puzzle: search the cans for a switch,
   then search a can next to it for the second one. Getting it wrong resets the first
   switch, so save once you have found it.
8. Three Trainers, then Lt. Surge: Voltorb 21, Pikachu 18, Raichu 24, all Electric. A
   Ground type is immune to everything he has except Voltorb's SonicBoom, which always
   does exactly 20. Do not bring anything Water or Flying.

The Thunder Badge raises Defense by 12.5%, whatever the game says on screen, and is
what allows Fly to be used outside battle once you have that HM.

Two things to do before moving on. Go back to `CeruleanCity` and trade the Bike Voucher
at `BikeShop` for the Bicycle. Then, because the next chapter is a pitch-dark cave, go
east to `Route11` and take `DiglettsCave`, which comes out on `Route2`: Oak's aide in
the `Route2` gatehouse gives you **HM05 (Flash)** once you have ten or more species in
the Pokédex.

Keep a Cut user in the party from here on. The way east out of `CeruleanCity` runs
through `CeruleanTrashedHouse` and the route beyond it is blocked by a tree.
