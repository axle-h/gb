# No badges: win the Boulder Badge

Goal: beat Brock in `PewterGym`.

Route: `RedsHouse2F` > `PalletTown` > **north** > `OaksLab` > `Route1` >
`ViridianCity` > back to `OaksLab` > `ViridianCity` > `Route2` > `ViridianForest` >
`Route2` > `PewterCity` > `PewterGym`.

1. You start upstairs in `RedsHouse2F`. Go down and out into `PalletTown`, then **walk
   north towards `Route1`**. Oak stops you at the top edge of town, tells you wild
   Pokémon in the grass are dangerous, and walks you to `OaksLab` himself.
   ⚠️ **Do this first, before going into the lab.** Until Oak has brought you in, the
   lab is a dead end that looks like a live one: the Poké Balls answer "Those are POKé
   BALLS" and hand over nothing, and the rival only says "Gramps isn't around!". No
   amount of talking to anyone in there changes it. Walking north is the trigger.
2. Now in `OaksLab`, once Oak has asked you to choose, take one of the three Poké Balls
   on the table: Charmander (Fire), Squirtle (Water) or Bulbasaur (Grass/Poison), left
   to right, all level 5. You get one and the other two are gone for good. Then beat
   the rival, who takes whichever starter beats yours.
3. Go north through `Route1` to `ViridianCity`. The clerk in `ViridianMart` gives you
   Oak's Parcel.
4. Carry it back to `OaksLab` and give it to Oak. He gives you the Pokédex.
   **Until the parcel is delivered an old man blocks the north exit of
   `ViridianCity`**, so there is no way to `Route2` and no way forward at all.
5. Return north and go through `Route2` into `ViridianForest`. Catch a few Pokémon and
   fight the Bug Catchers. Metapod and Kakuna know only Harden and cannot hurt you, so
   they are safe experience; about level 11 is enough for the gym.
6. Leave the forest north into `PewterCity`. Walking east to `Route3` will not work
   yet: a boy turns you back towards the gym.
7. In `PewterGym`, beat the Jr. Trainer, then Brock: Geodude level 12, Onix level 14,
   both Rock/Ground. Grass and Water moves hit them four times as hard. Electric moves
   do nothing, because Ground is immune.

The Boulder Badge raises Attack by 12.5% and is what allows Flash to be used outside
battle, once you find the HM for it.

Optional, and only until Brock is beaten: the rival waits west of `ViridianCity` on
`Route22` with a Pidgey at 9 and his starter at 8. After the badge he is gone from
there for good.
