# 4 badges: win the Soul Badge

Goal: beat Koga in `FuchsiaGym`. First finish the Pokémon Tower, which is what gets you
the Poké Flute, and the Poké Flute is what unblocks the road south.

Route: `LavenderTown` > `PokemonTower1F` up to `PokemonTower7F` > `LavenderTown` > `Route12` >
`Route13` > `Route14` > `Route15` > `FuchsiaCity` > `FuchsiaGym`.

1. **Look in the bag for the Silph Scope before you set foot in the tower, and if it is
   not there, go and get it first.** It is not in Silph Co, whatever the name suggests:
   it is at the bottom of the Rocket Hideout, under the Celadon Game Corner. In
   `GameCorner`, beat the Rocket standing in front of the poster at the back of the
   room and then examine the poster, which opens a staircase into `RocketHideoutB1F`.
   Two of the floors below are arrow tiles that slide you until something stops you;
   the action menu's routes already know where every slide ends, so do not try to work
   them out. Go down to `RocketHideoutB4F`, beat the Rocket there, and **talk to him a
   second time** — his
   after-battle line is what drops the **Lift Key**, and until you have heard it there
   is nothing to pick up. Then back up to `RocketHideoutB2F` and board
   `RocketHideoutElevator` there, not on `RocketHideoutB1F`, whose lift door stays shut
   until the Rocket guarding it is beaten. Ride to `RocketHideoutB4F`. That lands you
   in a room walled off from Giovanni; beat both Rockets in it and the wall comes
   down. Giovanni has Onix 25, Rhyhorn 24 and Kangaskhan 29, and leaves the
   **Silph Scope** on the floor when he goes. Ride the lift back to `RocketHideoutB2F`
   on the way out: `RocketHideoutB1F` is two halves split by a wall, only the northern
   one has the `GameCorner` staircase, and the lift's stop there is in the southern.
2. `PokemonTower1F` is the cave-looking entrance in the north-east of `LavenderTown`.
   The rival fights you on 2F, and floors 3F to 6F are Channelers whose Gastly and
   Haunter are Ghost/Poison. On 5F there are healing tiles in the middle of the floor,
   usable as often as you like.
3. On 6F a level 30 Marowak attacks as you approach the stairs. **You cannot fight it
   without the Silph Scope**; without one the game will not let you past at all.
4. Beat the three Rockets on 7F and talk to Mr. Fuji, who takes you home and gives you
   the **Poké Flute**.
5. Go south from `LavenderTown` on to `Route12`. **A sleeping Snorlax blocks the path.**
   Stand next to it and use the Poké Flute from the bag to wake it into a battle at
   level 30; it is worth catching, and there are only two in the game.
6. Keep south and west through `Route13`, `Route14` and `Route15` into `FuchsiaCity`.
   These are long routes with a lot of Trainers on them.
7. A second Snorlax on `Route16` blocks the other road into `FuchsiaCity`, which also
   needs the Bicycle. It is the longer way round, not the shorter.
8. `FuchsiaGym` has invisible walls; the way through is up the east side of the room.
   Koga has Koffing 37, Muk 39, Koffing 37 and Weezing 43, all pure Poison, so Psychic
   and Ground moves hit hard. The gym's other Trainers are mostly Psychic, which only
   Bug beats, so a strong neutral attack is usually the better answer. Weezing knows
   Selfdestruct and Toxic.

The Soul Badge raises Speed by 12.5% and is what allows Surf to be used outside battle.
HM03 (Surf) itself is in the Safari Zone, which is the next chapter, so take a Pokémon
that can learn Surf with you.
